Python tooling and SystemVerilog testbenches that run student hardware designs in
simulation and on real FPGAs, then grade them.

- Built the tooling that drives student designs through Vivado's simulator and onto
  physical boards, automating debugging, testing, and grading.
- Write exam questions and lab materials on digital logic, CMOS, finite state machines,
  pipelining, memory hierarchy, and FPGA design, under Prof. Woodward Yang.
- Run individualized design reviews, walking students through waveform analysis and
  their own SystemVerilog implementations.
