Most of my work happens at the boundary between a chip and the code driving it:
reduced-precision arithmetic units headed for tapeout, a rover's distributed control
system, a network driver written against the hardware registers.

Right now I'm in Harvard's Architecture, Circuits, and Compilers group under David
Brooks and Gu-Yeon Wei, teaching the university's computing hardware course, and
leading software for the robotics club's competition rover.
